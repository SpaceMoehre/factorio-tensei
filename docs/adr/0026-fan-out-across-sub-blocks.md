# A Side Input's belts into several Sub-Blocks share belts from the west edge

In Bands (ADR 0025) every Part took its Side Input on a belt of its own from the west edge. Py vrauks at 78 paddocks: each Part takes native flora and moss merged on one belt, a lane each, so 13 belts came in from the west edge, 698 a minute of each item in all. Two yellow belts carry that. A Fan-out (ADR 0011) fed only copies of one column. A Side Input that one belt cannot carry to several Sub-Blocks also came as its own route to each, and each route came on its own belts.

Now such routes, one to each consumer, share lines across Sub-Blocks once their copies stand. The routes must carry one item, or the same two merged. Their entries are taken in columns: each entry joins the column of the one west of it if it stands within twice FAN_REACH (16) columns of it. The line runs down the first column, up the next, and so on. The order is cut into runs like a column's: the fewest belts from the west edge, then the fewest splitters. Among equal cuts, the shortest lines win, counting from the west edge to each run's first entry and to each joining belt, and from entry to entry between. Belts already in a Fan-out of their Sub-Block's copies keep it.

- **Splitter spots.** In the 4 columns between the west edge and the west Parts, the water barrels', cocoons' and saps' links run down to their Parts. They are routed first. Only on a curve, or under those belts, does a belt reach an entry there. Before, each Part's own belt came straight in from the west edge on its row. A splitter used to stand only right before its entry's row, facing along the line, or right before the entry, facing east. Across Sub-Blocks it may now also stand anywhere up to 8 columns west of the entry and 4 rows above or below it. It may face any way and send the belt off from either side. The spots whose belts are shortest are tried first: to the entry, from the line, and on to the next entry. At 78 paddocks the line past the six west Parts finds a way only through these spots; before, only its two halves did. A copies' run keeps the old spots. With the new ones, Py fertilizer's 67 agitators in 116 × 116 came in on 44 belts, not 48. But most runs of copies fail either way, and trying every spot made the search far slower: Py science pack 2 took 340 s, not 40. Capped at the 8 cheapest, the spots missed the one the west Parts' line needs.
- **No way on.** Wherever a splitter's line has no way on (ahead, to either side or under), it is given up at once. Before, the next entry's spots were all tried first.
- **Routing.** Where Fan-outs take the room a link needs, the layout is routed again with them last. If that fails, it is routed without the Fan-outs across Sub-Blocks, each Sub-Block's belts coming from the west edge on their own as before. In the search, it is then routed without splitters, as before. Bands never routes without splitters.

Py vrauks in the 236 × 236 City Block of roboports, 78 paddocks: native flora and moss come in on 2 belts, not 13, through 11 splitters. One belt feeds the six west Parts (413 moss and 328 native flora a minute). The other feeds the top west row's second half and the six east Parts (285 and 370). Maximize finds the layout in about 10 s, not 49 s.

What still does not share: belts whose items differ (a Part's merged belt shares only with others carrying the same two items), and copies of one Sub-Block in several columns (ADR 0011). Placement keeps no room for a line: in the 4 columns beside the west Parts it takes what the other links leave.
