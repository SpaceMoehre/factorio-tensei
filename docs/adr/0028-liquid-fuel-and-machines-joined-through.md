# Liquid Fuel, machines joined through, mirrored machines and byproducts sorted out

Py's glassworks, smelters and others burn a fluid. The catalog folded fluids with a fuel value into the Fuel items and left the machines' energy source out, so a glassworks was planned as electric and never fed.

- **Liquid Fuel.** The catalog keeps fluids with a fuel value apart (`fluidFuels`). A machine burning a fluid records its effectivity, the fluid its energy source takes where it takes only one (`fuelFilter`), and that box as its last fluid box (production `fuel`). The recipe's fluids never take it. The plan adds the fuel as a fluid input: the power draw over the fuel value and effectivity, while the machine works, like a burner's Fuel. It comes by train on a pipe of its own, into the fuel box. The user chooses it (Liquid Fuel, natural gas by default). The app shows Fuel only where a machine of the chain burns an item, and Liquid Fuel only where one burns a fluid of the user's choice. A machine heated by its fluid's temperature (`burns_fluid` false: Py's TURD compost plants) is not supported.
- **Machines joined through.** The catalog keeps whether a connection lets fluid through both ways (`flow_direction` input-output, Factorio 2.0's default). Where a fluid's box has such connections on a machine's west and east sides, in line, the machines of a row stand against each other and join through them, as in the game. The row takes the fluid by one connection, east of its last machine, and keeps no column for it between them. Not where another fluid's connection on a side needs a column, or a gap stands between the machines. Before, a column of pipe stood between every two glassworks. Eight glassworks making flasks stand in two rows of four, each fed natural gas once.
- **Mirrored machines.** A machine may stand mirrored (Factorio 2.0): its fluid connections and drop point flipped east to west before it turns. The ways a machine stands are its rotations, then mirrored where that puts its connections and drop where no rotation does. The blueprint marks the machine `mirror`. A mirrored design is kept only where it starves less or is smaller than every unmirrored one that starves no more: otherwise its twins crowded out the other kinds of design (Py science pack 2's research centres came out in one row 901 wide).
- **Byproducts sorted out in the search.** Where a producer's belt carries an item none of its consumers takes, and it leaves on the train (Py's quartz crushers make stone with the crushed quartz), a filter splitter right after the producers sends that item on to the east edge. The rest goes on as one belt through the consumers (ADR 0027's filter, now outside Bands too). Before, it rode through every consumer and could fill their belt. Where the splitter finds no way, the producers' belt is chained through the consumers as before, route by route. A layout whose byproduct rides through its consumers ranks below one that sorts it out, after a Recipe Loop fed by train.

Still: one byproduct per belt is sorted out; a belt carrying two rides on through its consumers. Machines join through only along a row, not between rows.
